## Key Features

1. **Autonomous AI Voice & Conversational Agents:**
   - Sub-500ms voice response latency powered by streaming speech-to-text, low-latency LLM inference, and natural neural TTS.
   - Handles inbound qualification, objection handling, pricing questions, and live calendar booking without human handoff.
2. **RAG-Powered Knowledge Grounding:**
   - Vector-indexed product documentation ensures every answer is grounded in verified pricing, SLAs, and compliance facts, minimizing hallucinations.
3. **Automated Lead Scoring & Sentiment Intelligence:**
   - Extracts structured lead metadata (name, company, contact info, budget, timeline) and calculates a 0–100 lead score with sentiment classification (`positive`, `neutral`, `negative`).
4. **Instant Appointment Scheduling:**
   - Checks real-time slot availability, books discovery calls directly during the conversation, and prevents double-booking.
5. **Bi-Directional CRM Sync & Call Summaries:**
   - Generates concise post-call summaries and syncs transcripts, scores, and booked meetings automatically to your CRM.

## Onboarding Timeline

Fortivex AI Suite is engineered for rapid time-to-value with minimal IT overhead:

- **Day 1 — Workspace Provisioning & Kickoff (30 mins):**
  - Account setup, team invites, and ingestion of your product PDFs, FAQs, and pricing sheets into the knowledge base.
- **Days 2–3 — Playbook & CRM Configuration:**
  - Configure qualification criteria (budget thresholds, target industries, booking rules) and connect CRM + calendar webhooks.
- **Days 4–5 — Sandbox Testing & Voice Calibration:**
  - Test call flows with your sales leads, refine tone and objection-handling prompts, and validate lead-scoring thresholds.
- **Day 6–7 — Production Go-Live:**
  - Activate live inbound/outbound routing with real-time monitoring from our onboarding specialist.
- **Enterprise Custom Rollout:** Typically 2–3 weeks when SSO, custom VPC networking, or bespoke ERP integrations are included.

## Security & Compliance

Security and data privacy are built into every layer of Fortivex AI Suite:

- **Certifications & Attestations:**
  - **SOC 2 Type II** audited controls for security, availability, and confidentiality.
  - **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** certified Information Security Management System (ISMS).
- **Data Encryption:**
  - **In Transit:** TLS 1.3 for all API, WebSocket, and audio streams.
  - **At Rest:** AES-256 encryption for databases, vector indexes, and call recordings.
- **Data Residency & Privacy:**
  - Configurable data residency (India Mumbai region, EU Frankfurt, US East) and GDPR / DPDP Act (India) alignment.
  - Customer conversations and proprietary knowledge bases are **never** used to train public foundation models.
- **Access & Governance:**
  - Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), audit logs for all admin actions, and SAML 2.0 SSO (Enterprise).
